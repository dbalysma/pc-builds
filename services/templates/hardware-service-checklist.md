# Hardware service checklist

*Blank template. Copy it into the private client record before filling it in. Never commit a filled-in copy.*

**Job number:** DBPC-____-____ **Date:** ____

## Before opening
- [ ] Shut down fully; mains switch off; cable unplugged
- [ ] Power button held for a few seconds to drain remaining power
- [ ] Static control: wrist strap or regular grounding on bare metal; work on a non-static surface
- [ ] Interior photographed before anything is moved (private evidence)

## Handling
- [ ] Parts held by their edges; nothing touches contacts or chips
- [ ] Removed parts stored in anti-static bags and labelled with the job number
- [ ] Screws kept by location

## Cleaning (where in scope)
- [ ] Fans held still while cleaning, so they aren't spun by air
- [ ] CPU cooler and heatsink
- [ ] Case fans
- [ ] Graphics card heatsink and fans
- [ ] Dust filters and intake mesh
- [ ] Case interior
- [ ] No liquids on electronics

## Installation and reassembly
- [ ] Memory: modules in the slots the board manual specifies for their number; fully seated and latched
- [ ] Expansion cards: fully seated and secured
- [ ] Power connectors: every connector fully home (24-pin, CPU, GPU, drives)
- [ ] Display cable will be connected to the graphics card, not the motherboard, where a card is fitted
- [ ] Cables routed clear of every fan
- [ ] Nothing loose inside the case
- [ ] Interior photographed after the work (private evidence)

## Old parts
- [ ] Removed parts returned to the customer, or disposed of with their agreement (record which)
